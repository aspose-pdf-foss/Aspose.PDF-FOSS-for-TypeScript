# AutoTag semantic inference

Keep automatic font ranking as the default. Offer bookmark-title matching on the destination page, with bookmark depth determining heading level, and caller-defined descending font-size thresholds. Use existing list-marker and indentation rules to recognize lists in reading order and attach links to their item text. Keep ambiguous prose readable.

Repeated furniture and watermarks are opt-in. Reuse the document's recurrence detector, restrict running headers and footers to the margins, and require positive watermark evidence for central content. Preserve the ink and avoid assigning one draw to competing structure elements.

Verify structural roles, marked content, link ownership, false positives and reading order after saving and reopening a representative corpus.
